1. Introduction

Welcome to Glacier Puck Strike! This Privacy Policy explains how Alma Mia Glow Spa LLC ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in connection with the Glacier Puck Strike mobile application for Android devices.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Glacier Puck Strike is built as a standalone, offline arcade sports game. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, device identifiers, or sensitive information from our users.

2. Information Collection and Use

Personal Data

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, telephone number, or billing details). You do not need to register an account, sign in, or provide any credentials to enjoy the game.

Device and Hardware Data

Glacier Puck Strike does not collect hardware serial numbers, IMEI, MAC addresses, advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA), or phone state data.

Location Data

We do not request, track, or access your precise or approximate GPS location.

Diagnostic & Usage Analytics

The application does not integrate third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics for Firebase, Flurry, or Unity Analytics tracking). We do not record your in-game behavior, session durations, or gameplay telemetry.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

  • Zero Advertising: Glacier Puck Strike contains no third-party banner ads, interstitial ads, rewarded video ads, or ad networks.
  • Zero Third-Party SDKs: The game operates without external tracking libraries, social network plugins, or third-party marketing SDKs.
  • No Data Sharing or Sale: Because no data is collected, we never sell, trade, license, or share user data with any third parties.

4. Device Permissions

Glacier Puck Strike requests only basic permissions strictly necessary for rendering graphics and playing sound on your Android device:

  • No Sensitive Permissions Requested: We do not request access to your Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Calendar, Storage/Media, Location, or SMS.
  • Full Offline Play: The game runs 100% offline and does not require an active internet connection to play.

5. Local Data Storage and Retention

Any game settings (such as audio volume or highest score) are stored exclusively locally on your device using Unity's native local preferences (PlayerPrefs). This data:

  • Remains solely on your physical device.
  • Is never transmitted over the internet or uploaded to any server or cloud service.
  • Can be permanently deleted at any time by clearing the app's cache/data via your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of young children is especially important to us. Glacier Puck Strike is an all-ages, family-friendly arcade game:

  • We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union / EEA).
  • In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), no personal data of any kind is requested or collected from any user, regardless of age.
  • If a parent or guardian believes their child has somehow provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will ensure no such information exists.

7. Data Security

Because Glacier Puck Strike does not transmit or store personal information over the internet, your data is completely protected from remote server vulnerabilities or data breaches.
